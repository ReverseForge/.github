# 🔒 Security Policy for ReverseForge

**Last Updated:** May 10, 2026

## 📡 Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a vulnerability in one of our tools, or wish to report a security concern, please email us directly at:

📧 **`team@reverseforge.dev`**

We guarantee acknowledgement within **48 hours** and will work with you on a timeline for resolution and coordinated public disclosure. 

We strongly prefer **English** communication, but can handle technical discussions in our native language if needed.

## 🛡️ Our Disclosure Philosophy

ReverseForge practices **Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD)**. We firmly believe that:

1.  **Vendors must have time to patch.** Our standard disclosure deadline is **90 days** from the date of initial notification. The clock may be extended by mutual agreement for complex fixes.
2.  **The public has a right to know.** If a vendor becomes unresponsive or the deadline passes, we reserve the right to publish full technical details to protect end-users through transparency.
3.  **Proof-of-Concept is not optional.** We provide working PoC code or detailed exploitation steps in all our advisories. Security through obscurity is not acceptable.

## 📜 Policy on AI-Assisted Discovery

As of 2026, with the rise of AI-driven vulnerability discovery tools like Project Glasswing and autonomous pentesting agents, **we believe in full transparency regarding tooling**:
- Any vulnerability discovered or significantly analyzed with AI assistance will be explicitly noted in the advisory.
- We never submit AI-generated reports without thorough human validation and manual root-cause analysis.

## ⚖️ Legal Safe Harbor

We conduct all research in good faith. If you are a vendor and believe our disclosure process requires adjustment, please contact us immediately. We have never engaged in extortion, and we never will. Our goal is a safer internet.

**ReverseForge — Forging exploit intelligence, responsibly.**
